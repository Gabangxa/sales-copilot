# Voice and style

Edit this file so drafts sound like you. Point to your own copy with `--style path/to/file.md` or `STYLE_PATH`.

- Clear, confident and courteous. Senior but not stiff.
- Open with the person's name and get to the point in the first sentence.
- Refer to the client's own words and priorities from the call.
- Use specific numbers, currency pairs and dates when they were discussed. Never round or embellish them.
- Recap next steps as a short "-" list when there are two or more.
- One call to action, phrased as an easy yes (for example "Would Thursday at 10:00 work for the pricing walkthrough?").
- British spelling (organise, prioritise, colour).
- No hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "synergy", "circle back", "touch base".

## Example of an email I liked

Hi Thandi,

Thank you for the time this morning. It was useful to hear how the team is handling the USD/NGN exposure on the import book, and that settlement delays are the main frustration rather than pricing.

As agreed:
- I will send indicative forward pricing for 1 and 3 month tenors by Thursday.
- You will confirm whether treasury or procurement signs off on new counterparties.

Would Tuesday at 14:00 work for a 20 minute walkthrough of the pricing with your CFO?
